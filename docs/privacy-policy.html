Privacy Policy

Dress Inventory · Effective July 5, 2026

Overview

Dress Inventory (“the app”) is a wardrobe management app published by Hanzel. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

The app does not collect, transmit, or share your personal data with us or any third party. All information you enter stays on your device.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following data locally in your phone’s private app storage:

  • Wardrobe items you add (name, category, clothing type, and colour)
  • Outfit wear history (dates and which items you logged as worn)
  • Shopping wishlist colours you select

This data is saved in a file on your device (closet.json in the app’s private storage). It is not uploaded to our servers because the app has no network access.

What we do not collect

  • No account registration or login
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs
  • No location data
  • No contacts, messages, or other device data outside what you enter in the app
  • No data sent over the internet — the app does not request network permissions

Permissions

The app does not request network, location, contacts, or other sensitive device permissions. All features work offline using only local storage on your device.

Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Uninstalling Dress Inventory removes the app’s private storage from your device.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Because no data leaves the device, there is no age-based data collection.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The “Effective” date at the top will reflect the latest version. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at github.com/hanzel1698/dress-inventory/issues or open an issue on the project repository.
